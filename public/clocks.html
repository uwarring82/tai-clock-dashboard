Physikalisch-Technische Bundesanstalt · Germany

PTB-CSF1

Caesium fountain · Primary frequency standard

PTB uses this fountain in the realization of UTC(PTB) and for calibration of the TAI scale interval.

Published systematic uncertainty
2.74 × 10−16

Weyers et al. (2018), table 2. Standard primary-frequency operation.

Read the PTB evaluation

Physikalisch-Technische Bundesanstalt · Germany

PTB-CSF2

Caesium fountain · Primary frequency standard

PTB alternates between two atom densities to evaluate the frequency shift caused by collisions between cold caesium atoms.

Published systematic uncertainty
1.71 × 10−16

Weyers et al. (2018), table 2. Standard primary-frequency operation.

Read the PTB evaluation

National Institute of Standards and Technology · United States

NIST-F4

Caesium fountain · Primary frequency standard

Built using parts of NIST-F1, this fountain probes the microwave transition in laser-cooled caesium atoms.

Published systematic uncertainty
2.2 × 10−16

Gerginov et al. evaluation (2025), with the corrected uncertainty budget in the 2026 corrigendum.

Read the NIST evaluation · Corrigendum

National Institute of Information and Communications Technology · Japan

NICT-Sr1

Strontium optical lattice clock · Secondary frequency standard

This clock probes the 698 nm transition in strontium-87. NICT began contributing its calibrations to TAI in December 2018.

Published systematic uncertainty
5.4 × 10−17

Nemitz et al. (2021), table 1. Nominal clock budget before the gravitational redshift correction.

Clock histories and weights

The profiles use dated laboratory evaluations. Frequency standards can calibrate the rate of TAI, while the ensemble uses clock phase records to form the free atomic scale. We have no official BIPM endorsement or clearance to reuse submitted laboratory records. Individual phase histories and ensemble weights await documented reuse clearance.
