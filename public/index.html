National realizations of UTC

National laboratories maintain real-time signals called UTC(k). The offsets below are BIPM comparisons for one shared epoch. The service links open the institutes’ own clocks or network time services.

31 October 2026, 00:00 UTC · MJD 61344
Local time scale UTC − UTC(k) Official time service
UTC(PTB)Germany −0.1 nsu = 1.3 ns PTB web clock
UTC(NIST)United States +0.1 nsu = 2.1 ns NIST web clock
UTC(NPL)United Kingdom −0.3 nsu = 2.7 ns NPL network time
UTC(NICT)Japan +0.2 nsu = 2.3 ns NICT web clock

A positive offset means the local realization is behind UTC. u is the published combined standard uncertainty. Source BIPM Circular T 466, §1, issued 13 November 2026. For newer provisional comparisons, see Rapid UTC, which uses UTCr as its reference.
